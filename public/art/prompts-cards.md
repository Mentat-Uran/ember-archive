# Card cover prompts

Each image was generated as an independent asset with the built-in `image_gen` tool (one prompt and one call per card; no contact sheet). `transparent_background=false` for every call. The shared art direction is included below; each individual prompt adds a single card-specific object/action.

## Shared art direction

Original, restrained dark fantasy game-card cover illustration. Flat 2D cut-paper screenprint with clean, simple silhouettes and very little texture; solemn, not realistic, not 3D. Uniform near-black charcoal-gray background. Use only two or three broad color blocks from old bone white, muted amber, dark red, and iron gray. One clear central object/action, readable at small card size, square or slightly portrait composition. No lettering, numbers, runes, logos, UI, card frame, watermark, people, extra objects, or recognizable existing-IP designs. No busy scenery.
